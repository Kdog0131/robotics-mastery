What a for loop does and when to use it: A for loop is useful for when you want to repeat multiple lines of code multiple times.

What a while loop does and when to use it: A while loop keeps running so long as a condition is true, and it stops running when the condition becomes false.

What overshoot means and why step size matters: Overshoot means that your Robot has went past its intended target because the step size was too large.

The range() function - what it starts at and stops at: range(n) starts at 0 and stops BEFORE n. range (5) makes it go a total of five times - 0,1,2,3,4 and does not litterally stop at 5.
