Robot I picked: Nasa's Curiosity Rover

SENSE -> It utelizes camras and sensors for movement and decision making. Specifically a Mastcam(stereo color cameras), Hazcams(hazard avoidence), Navcam(Navigation), ChemCam(Fires a laser at rocks to vaporize them and to read the chemical signature)
THINK -> It figures out what samples to collect and how to collect them
ACT -> In the real world it can grab and examine objects that its programmed to collect.
POWER -> It is powered by a Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator.

One thing that would break it if it failed: No power.
